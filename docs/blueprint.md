# **App Name**: Grison Club Modern

## Core Features:

- Hero Section with Dynamic Content: Display a captivating background image or video showcasing young people in action, overlaid with the organization's slogan: "Formé, Inspirer, Agir pour Demain." Dynamically update content to reflect current campaigns.
- "Who We Are" Section: Showcase the association's vision of social transformation and community service using engaging visuals and concise text. The section further presents the organization's 3 pillars with the assistance of an AI tool to help new members or visitors navigate content easier.
- "Our Clubs" Directory: Feature affiliated branches such as Grison Médecins, Grison Avocats, and Grison Youth, allowing visitors to browse and find clubs of interest.
- "Membership" Process: Detail the membership process, emphasizing key steps from application submission to validation, with visually appealing progress indicators. Also include pricing: 50,000 GNF joining fee and 200,000 GNF annual subscription.
- "News/Events" Section: Feature latest news and upcoming events like reforestation campaigns or seminars with interactive calendar and RSVP functionality.
- Contact and Pre-Adhesion Forms: Implement contact and pre-adhesion forms allowing interested individuals to reach out or begin the membership process.
- "Donations" Button: Include a clearly labeled "Donations" button, allowing visitors to contribute via Caisse Indépendante.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A vibrant blue (#29ABE2) to represent dynamism, youth, and trustworthiness.
- Background color: Light grey (#F0F0F0) provides a clean and modern backdrop.
- Accent color: A bright green (#90EE90) complements the blue, highlighting action items and calls to action.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif, combines a modern look and a little warmth or personality; suitable for headlines or body text.
- Use a modern, flat icon set related to education, culture, and environmental themes. Icons should be consistently styled throughout the site.
- Employ a grid-based layout with generous white space to provide a clean, organized user experience. Use clear section dividers and intuitive navigation.
- Incorporate subtle animations such as transitions and hover effects to provide a dynamic and engaging feel, ensuring they enhance usability and do not distract from the content.
